OC Role · 05

OCVP Delegates

Recruit, communicate with and serve every delegate who walks into LeaCon II.

Applications open Deadline: 10 October 2026, 11.59 pm 2 vacancies Reports to OCP directly Term 26.27
Apply for this role Read expectations
01

Job description

Delegates owns the people side of LeaCon II. You prepare the delegate servicing requirements and the delegate timeline, prepare and send delegate mailers, and handle delegate communication before, during and after the event — including international delegates where relevant.

You reach school students, school leavers and undergraduates to grow registrations, manage registrations accurately and on time, maintain active communication with the relevant leadership, and ensure a quality delegate experience throughout LeaCon II.

02

Key responsibilities

  1. Prepare delegate servicing requirements and the delegate timeline.
  2. Prepare and send delegate mailers.
  3. Handle delegate communication before, during and after the event.
  4. Maintain active communication with the relevant leadership.
  5. Contact international delegates where relevant.
  6. Reach school students, school leavers and undergraduates to grow registrations.
  7. Manage registrations accurately and on time.
  8. Ensure a quality delegate experience throughout LeaCon II.
  9. Update the conference tracker and participate in pre-event and post-event meetings.
03

What you will learn

  1. Audience building and recruitment outreach.
  2. Lifecycle communication — mailers, follow-ups and reminders.
  3. Registration and data management.
  4. Service and experience design for delegates.
  5. Collaboration with Marketing on messaging.
04

Working style & commitment

  1. Full-term commitment from selection through to the post-event report — no mid-event withdrawal (Policy 01).
  2. Communication-heavy role with clear response times.
  3. Attends pre-event and post-event meetings; keeps the conference tracker current.
  4. Works through the defined hierarchy with leadership (Policy 05).
