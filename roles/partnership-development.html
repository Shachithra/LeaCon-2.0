OC Role · 02

OCVP Partnership Development

Raise the funding, gift partners and financial reporting that make LeaCon II possible.

Applications open Deadline: 10 October 2026, 11.59 pm 4 vacancies Reports to OCP & Event Manager Term 26.27
Apply for this role Read expectations
01

Job description

Partnership Development owns the money behind LeaCon II. You raise the funding that covers the event's budgeted expenses, and secure the gift partners required for the competition and recognition requirements of the event — turning a plan into confirmed commitments.

Day to day you build fundraising strategies and carry them through with the relevant stakeholders, own the financial report, and handle post-event partner follow-up — dues, reports and appreciation — working closely with BD, Finance and LC leadership.

02

Key responsibilities

  1. Raise money to cover the budgeted event expenses of LeaCon II.
  2. Raise gift partners for the competition and recognition requirements of the event.
  3. Create fundraising strategies and carry them through with relevant stakeholders.
  4. Maintain active communication with the relevant BD, Finance and leadership stakeholders.
  5. Prepare the financial report; finalize and submit financial reporting on time.
  6. Send a project report and an appreciation token to partners after the event.
  7. Collect partner dues after the event.
  8. Update the conference tracker and participate in pre-event and post-event meetings.
03

What you will learn

  1. Fundraising strategy and pitch practice — building the case and asking with confidence.
  2. Negotiation and partner relationship management.
  3. Financial planning, budgeting and reporting discipline.
  4. Cross-functional work with BD and Finance.
  5. Post-event accountability — dues, reports and partner appreciation.
04

Working style & commitment

  1. Full-term commitment from selection through to the post-event report — no mid-event withdrawal (Policy 01).
  2. Targets and deadlines drive the week — the budget does not wait.
  3. Present at regular pre-event and post-event meetings; keeps the conference tracker updated.
  4. Communicates promptly through the defined hierarchy (Policy 03 / 05).
