Application guidelines

How it works.

  1. 01 Explore OC roles Browse the five role briefs and note your preferences.
  2. 02 Read expectations & policies Understand what OC membership requires of you.
  3. 03 Choose preferences Pick a first and second choice among the five OC roles.
  4. 04 Complete application Fill all five steps accurately before you submit.
  5. 05 Screening Applications are reviewed against the role requirements.
  6. 06 Interview Selected candidates are invited to an interview.
  7. 07 Selection Final decisions are shared with all applicants.
Before you submit

Read the job descriptions, expectations and policies thoroughly. Complete all application sections accurately. Submit before the deadline. Interview information will be shared with selected candidates.

Before you begin

  • Read the five role briefs, plus the expectations and policies sections.
  • Have a professional photo ready — JPG or PNG, up to 3 MB.
  • Use an email and WhatsApp number you check regularly.
  • You can review every answer on the final step before submitting.

After you apply

  • Screening — applications reviewed against role requirements.
  • Interview — selected candidates are invited.
  • Selection — final decisions shared with everyone.
